# My Favorite Movie: I'm Drunk, I Love You

## About the Movie

**I'm Drunk, I Love You** is a Filipino romantic comedy-drama film released in 2017, directed by **J.P. Habac**. It tells the story of two college best friends, Carson and Dio, who go on a road trip to La Union before graduation.

### Main Characters
- **Carson** - A college student who has been secretly in love with her best friend for years.
- **Dio** - Carson's best friend, who loves music and is about to graduate.
- **Pathy** - Carson's close friend who supports her throughout the story.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because it portrays unrequited love, friendship, and the difficulty of expressing true feelings. It shows that sometimes, loving someone means accepting that they may not feel the same way.

> Sometimes, the hardest part of love is accepting that the person you love may never love you back.

## My Favorite Things About the Movie

1. The realistic and relatable story.
2. The beautiful scenery of La Union.
3. The friendship between Carson and Dio.
4. The emotional lessons about love and moving on.

## My Rating

**Rating: 10/10** ⭐

### Lessons I Learned

- Be brave enough to express your feelings.
- Appreciate the people who are always there for you.
- Accept that not every love story has a happy ending.
- Learn to let go and move forward.

*I'm Drunk, I Love You is a memorable Filipino film that reminds us that love is not always about being together, but also about learning, growing, and letting go.*
